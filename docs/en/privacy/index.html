Privacy policy

This page describes the current Piss on Trees 0.2.22 beta for iPhone and iPad. The main game runs locally. Online scores through Apple Game Center are optional.

Developer and data contact: Sergei Travinov. GitHub

Progress and custom parks

Settings, the saved walk, local records, run history, imported maps and their images are stored in the app’s data on your device. The game has no developer-run account server or park-upload service.

Import processes the map file you choose. When you share a file with another app, you choose its recipients. System backups are controlled by your device and iCloud settings.

Optional online scores

When you connect Game Center, the app uses your game-scoped Apple identifier and nickname, binds its score queue to that player, and submits the score of a completed built-in campaign. Custom parks and guest runs are excluded. Failed submissions remain on the device for that same player to retry.

Your nickname and score may be visible to other players on a global leaderboard. Apple operates Game Center under its Game Center privacy policy.

You can stop future submissions using the disable online records button in the records screen. This does not remove scores already sent to Apple. Manage your Apple profile and data through Game Center and your Apple Account.

Testing through TestFlight

Beta installation uses Apple TestFlight. Apple processes installation, session, crash and voluntarily submitted feedback information and makes available testing reports to the developer. Screenshots and comments you send are used to diagnose and fix issues.

See Apple TestFlight for details. You can stop testing in TestFlight.

Links on park objects

A custom map may include external links. The game shows the destination address and asks for confirmation before opening one. The destination website may receive your IP address and other ordinary browser information and applies its own data policy.

Support messages

If you send an email or feedback report, the developer receives your address, message and any files you choose to attach. This information is used to reply and diagnose the issue. Correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request; you may request deletion through the same contact channel.

Contact: GitHub. The developer has no remote access to saved data on your device.

GitHub issues are public and handled under the GitHub privacy statement. Use TestFlight for private details.

This website and analytics

The game includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising cookies, upload forms or localStorage. The email-copy button writes to the clipboard only when you press it.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for service security: GitHub Pages data collection.

Deletion and your choices

You can remove custom parks in My Parks. Deleting the app through iOS may delete its remaining local data. Exported files, system backups, email and data held by Apple must be managed separately with the relevant service.

Local play does not require Game Center. Disabling online records stops new submissions, but does not remove data already sent to Apple. This page is updated when features change.
