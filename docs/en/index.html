Before you go

Questions & answers

How do I play?
  1. Choose the walk button and start a walk.
  2. Use the on-screen arrows to move. Approach a tree and tap the mark button.
  3. Mark every tree while avoiding the owners with leashes. Trees can change position too.
What should I test?

Try the arrows, marking trees, movement on different surfaces, pursuit, pause, resuming a walk and sound. In a night park, check visibility within the flashlight and scent areas. In a park with a doghouse, try hiding and coming out.

A useful report includes the park number, build number, reproduction steps and a screenshot.

Why does movement speed change?

Asphalt, sand, gravel and wooden paths affect movement differently. A map can mix several surfaces.

What happens at night?

Trees and pursuers are hidden outside the owner’s flashlight and the dog’s scent area. The enhanced scent ability briefly reveals more.

How do I hide in a doghouse?

In a park with a doghouse, approach its entrance and use the action button. While the dog is hidden, the owners lose track and wander in search of it. Pursuit may resume after you come out.

How do I open my own park?

Save a .potlevel file in Files on your iPhone and open it with Piss on Trees. Imported parks appear in the My Parks section.

Map images and objects are stored on the device. An external link opens only after a warning showing its address and your confirmation.

Do I need an account?

No in-game registration is required for local walks. Game Center is an optional connection in the records screen.

Why is the Game Center leaderboard missing?

The online leaderboard is still being configured and tested. Availability depends on the build and Apple’s service. Version 0.2.22 checks whether the leaderboard loads and shows a message if it is unavailable. Local records work independently.

Will an update remove my parks?

Normal updates are designed to keep local parks and progress. Keep the original files of your own maps separately. Deleting the app may delete its local data.
